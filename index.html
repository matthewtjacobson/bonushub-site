Questions

What people ask first.

Is BonusHub a sportsbook or betting app?

No. BonusHub is a client service. The promotions themselves are offered by separate apps, and you open any account directly with that app under its own terms.

Is there a guaranteed return?

No. Every promotion has conditions set by its platform, and platforms can change or end an offer at any time. We explain what each one requires before you start, so you can decide with the full picture.

Who can work with BonusHub?

Adults who meet each platform's own eligibility rules. Age minimums and available states differ from app to app, and some require you to be 21 or older. We'll only suggest programs you qualify for.

How do I get paid?

You claim each bonus in your own account, and the platform pays it to you directly. Our fee and when it's due are agreed in writing before you begin, and every payment is recorded in your client ledger. You can ask for a statement at any time.

Do you put money in my account?

No. BonusHub never deposits into, funds, or logs into client accounts. You open and fund every account yourself, and you stay in control of it.

Someone referred me. Does that matter?

Yes. Put their name or code in the "Referred by" box on the form so they get credit.

What information will you ask for?

The inquiry form asks only for contact details. If a specific arrangement later needs identity verification, we'll explain why and how it's kept before collecting anything. Never send photos of your ID, account numbers or passwords through the form or in a DM.
